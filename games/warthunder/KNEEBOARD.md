## Check in flight

- **Elevator trim** — hat forward should drop the nose, the way it does in DCS and BMS. War Thunder names the steps by sign ("Positive"/"Negative") and the sign is not settled
- **Zoom** — the dial does not centre, so the view may start part-zoomed
- **View mini-stick** — your head should go where your thumb goes
